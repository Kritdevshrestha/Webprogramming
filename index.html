About Afnan

Afnan Perfumes is a UAE-based luxury fragrance house that blends the richness of Arabian perfumery with modern-day elegance. Established with the goal of creating distinctive and captivating scents, Afnan has quickly become one of the most sought-after brands in the global perfume industry.

Known for their long-lasting, high-quality ingredients, Afnan's perfumes cater to both men and women who value sophistication, individuality, and style. Signature collections like Supremacy, 9PM, and Rare represent the brand’s commitment to quality and innovation.

Each fragrance from Afnan tells a story — one of culture, confidence, and charm — offering a unique olfactory experience designed to leave a lasting impression.

Popular Perfumes

Supremacy
